WHAT YOU GET

Everything a service business needs to take bookings online.

The same system behind SnowHop, set up around your services, prices and schedule.

Online booking

Customers pick a day that works. You set how many jobs you take each day.

Customer accounts

Customers sign in with their phone number and a 4-digit PIN to see, pay for or cancel their visits.

Online payments

Customers pay by card, Apple Pay or Cash App through Stripe, or pay you after the job.

Prepaid credit

Sell credit packs up front. Credit comes off each new booking automatically.

Your owner page

See each day's jobs, mark them done and paid, look up customers and add credit, all from your phone.

Instant alerts

Get an email the moment someone books or cancels, with their address and how they're paying.
