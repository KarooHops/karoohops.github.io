Privacy Policy

What KarooHops collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Effective November 26, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to reply to you, run booking sites and get paid.
  • We don't sell personal information or use it for ads.
  • If you book with a business that uses KarooHops, that business sees your booking.
  • Email support@karoohops.com to see, fix or delete your information.

This is a summary. The full policy below is what counts.

1. Who we are

KarooHops LLC (“KarooHops,” “we” or “us”) is a Colorado company based in Highlands Ranch, Colorado. This policy covers karoohops.com, the SnowHop demo, and the booking websites and apps we run for businesses (“booking sites”).

2. What we collect

  • When you use our Get started form: your name, business name, phone number, email address, what you need and your message. To stop spam, we also save a scrambled (hashed) copy of your IP address and delete it within a few hours.
  • When you email us: your email address and what you write.
  • When your business becomes a customer: your business and contact details, the content you give us for your site (such as services, prices, logo and photos) and your billing records. Stripe handles card payments, so we never see your full card number.
  • When you use a booking site: what you enter, such as your name, phone number, email address, service address, booking dates and notes, messages to the business and reviews you leave, plus a record of your bookings, payments and prepaid credit. Your PIN is stored scrambled, never as you typed it. We look up the map location of your address to check that it's in the business's service area.
  • Automatically: when you load a page, our hosting and font providers receive technical details such as your IP address and browser type. karoohops.com doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. Booking sites remember a few things in your browser so you don't have to retype them, such as your sign-in, your booking details and your support chat. The SnowHop demo keeps what you enter in your browser only and erases it when you close the tab.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to you, send quotes and set up your site.
  • To run booking sites: taking bookings, sending booking emails and alerts, and handling payments and prepaid credit.
  • To bill customers and keep the records tax law requires.
  • To keep our systems secure, stop spam and abuse, and fix problems.

We don't sell personal information, we don't share it for advertising, and we don't send marketing emails unless you ask for them.

4. Who we share it with

  • The business you booked with. If you use a booking site, that business sees your booking details and decides how to use them in its work.
  • Service providers that help us run KarooHops, only for that purpose:
    • Supabase, for our database and servers
    • GitHub, which hosts karoohops.com
    • Stripe, for payments
    • Resend, which sends our emails
    • Google, for our email inbox (Gmail) and the fonts on our pages (Google Fonts). Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
    • Namecheap, for our domain and email forwarding
    • The U.S. Census Bureau and Zippopotam.us, which receive the address or ZIP code you enter on a booking site to find its location
    • The National Weather Service and Windy, on booking sites that show weather, like SnowHop. They receive your IP address when the forecast or radar loads.
    • Apple, if a business has an iPhone app, under Apple's own policies
    • Software and AI tools we use to build, maintain and support KarooHops
  • When the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or our rights.
  • If KarooHops changes hands. If KarooHops is sold or merged, your information may move to the new owner, still covered by this policy.

5. How long we keep it

  • Get started requests and emails: as long as we need them to follow up and for our records. Then we delete them.
  • Customer records: while you're a customer, and billing records for as long as tax law requires.
  • Booking-site information: while the business uses KarooHops. When its plan ends, the business can ask for a copy for 30 days, and then we delete the information.

Copies may stay in backups for a short time until the backups are replaced.

6. Your choices

  • Email support@karoohops.com to see, correct or delete your information, or to get a copy of it. We'll answer within 30 days. We may need to confirm it's you, and we may keep what the law requires us to keep, such as payment records.
  • If you booked through a business's site, you can also ask that business. We'll help it answer you.
  • Depending on where you live, state law may give you more rights. Email us to use them.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store PINs and sign-in tokens scrambled, and limit who can reach your information. No system is perfectly secure, so if a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

8. Children

KarooHops is for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes here with a new date, and we'll email customers about important ones.

10. Contact

Email support@karoohops.com with any question about your information or this policy.
